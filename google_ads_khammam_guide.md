## 1. Campaign Objective & Type

- **Campaign Goal:** Leads / Phone Calls
- **Campaign Type:** Search Campaign (with Call Assets) **OR** Dedicated **Call-Only Campaign**.
- **Device Target:** Mobile Phones (100% Mobile focus for emergency calls).

## 2. Precise Location Radius Setup (30 KM Radius around Khammam)

To ensure Mahesh covers his exact 30 km service radius around his hospital / Google Business Profile address:

1. In Google Ads, go to **Campaign Settings > Locations**.
2. Select **Radius** (instead of Location Name).
3. Enter Mahesh's address:
   `743Q+4XF, Station Rd, Mayuri Center, Mamillagudaem, Khammam, Telangana 507001`
   *(or drop a pin on Mayuri Center / Khammam Station Road)*.
4. Set the radius distance to **30 kilometers** (30 km).
5. Select **Location Options:**
   - Target: **"Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations"** *(This ensures you only pay for people physically located in the 30 km Khammam radius, avoiding wasted clicks from outside)*.

## 6. Linking Google Business Profile & Call Assets

1. Go to **Assets > Location Assets** in Google Ads.
2. Link the **Google Business Profile** access given by Mahesh (Station Rd, Mayuri Center, Mamillagudaem, Khammam 507001).
3. Go to **Assets > Call Assets**.
4. Add New Call Asset:
   - Country: **India (+91)**
   - Phone Number: **9640894489**
   - Turn ON **Call Reporting** to track call duration and count completed calls.

---

## 7. Conversion Tracking Setup

1. In Google Ads, go to **Tools & Settings > Conversions**.
2. Click **+ New Conversion Action** -> Select **Phone Calls**.
3. Choose: **Calls from ads using call assets or call-only ads**.
4. Set minimum call duration to **10 or 15 seconds** (so only genuine calls count as conversions).
5. Copy the Conversion ID & Label (e.g. `AW-XXXXXXXXXX` / `LABEL`), and update the environment variables or `lib/tracking.ts` file on the landing page website.

---

## 8. Bidding & Budget Strategy

- **Bidding Strategy:** Start with **Maximize Clicks** with a Maximum CPC Limit (e.g. ₹20–₹40 per click) or **Maximize Conversions** once call data starts coming in.
- **Daily Budget:** ₹300 – ₹500 / day to test initial lead flow.
- **Ad Schedule:** **24 Hours / 7 Days a week** (Emergency medical services run 24/7).
